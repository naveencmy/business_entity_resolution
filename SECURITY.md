## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of this project and the safety of our contributors and users seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability or sensitive data exposure issue, please follow these steps:

### 1. Do NOT Open a Public Issue
Please do not report vulnerabilities via public GitHub issues, discussions, or social media.

### 2. Contact the Maintainers Privately
Send an email to **naveencmy.dev@gmail.com** with:
- A descriptive subject line: `[SECURITY] Potential vulnerability in Business Entity Resolution Pipeline`.
- A detailed explanation of the vulnerability.
- Steps to reproduce or proof-of-concept (PoC) code.
- Impact assessment (e.g., arbitrary code execution, denial of service, data leakage).

### 3. Response Process
- **Acknowledgement:** We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours.
- **Assessment:** We will confirm the vulnerability and determine its impact and severity.
- **Fix & Release:** We will work on a fix in a private branch and coordinate a release.
- **Credit:** We will publicly acknowledge and credit you in the release notes (unless you prefer anonymity).

## Fair Play & Data Integrity Note
In accordance with competition rules:
- No external APIs, commercial entity resolution services, or government database lookups may be integrated.
- All dependencies must be open source (MIT/Apache 2.0).
